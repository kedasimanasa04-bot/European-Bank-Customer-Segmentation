# European Bank Customer Segmentation

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn and identifies customer segments that may require better retention strategies.

The analysis was performed using the European Bank customer dataset in Microsoft Excel using PivotTables, PivotCharts, and customer segmentation techniques.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall customer churn
- Study churn across different age groups
- Analyze churn by credit score
- Analyze customer tenure and churn
- Analyze churn by balance segment
- Compare churn across geographical regions
- Analyze churn by gender
- Study the relationship between engagement and product usage
- Identify high-value and disengaged customer segments

## Key Findings

- Overall customer churn rate: **20.37%**
- The **46–60 age group** has a churn rate of **51.12%**
- **Germany** has a churn rate of **32.44%**
- High-balance customers have a churn rate of **24.98%**
- Female customers have a churn rate of **25.07%**
- Customers with 3 products have an **82.71%** churn rate
- Customers with 4 products show **100%** churn in this dataset
- High-balance and inactive customers have a churn rate of **32.33%**
